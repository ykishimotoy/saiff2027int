Criteria

What we want to see is a story.

Which AI you used, or how new the technology is, is not the subject of the judging. What does the work say, what experience does it create, and how far does it move the audience? We look at the power of the story first.

01 — Story

What did you depict?

Why tell this story? What do you want to say? Has what lived inside the creator risen into being as a work?

02 — Experience

What experience did you create?

The design of image, sound, and time. On the big screen, in the darkness of the theater, does the work become an experience the audience can bathe in?

03 — Impact

How far did you move the heart?

Make them laugh. Make them cry. Surprise them. Make them think. Did you leave something with the viewer?

We look at technique insofar as it supports the story. How well the creator controlled the characteristic seams of AI, broken images or sound, inconsistencies in characters or worlds, and finished the work without compromising the experience. And the willingness to try new forms of expression. These are evaluated as secondary factors supporting the three points above.

The Selection Process

  1. First-Round Review

    All submitted films are reviewed.

  2. Second Selection — Confirmed screening at SCARTS

    Films that pass the Second Selection are announced on April 15, 2027 and screened at SCARTS Court and Studio A / B on the festival day (Selection Screening, from 10:00, free admission).

  3. Final Selection (Finalists) — Confirmed screening at the hitaru theater

    Films that pass the Final Selection are additionally screened on the big screen of Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru and become eligible for all awards.

The Second Selection and Final Selection films are announced together on April 15, 2027. The number of films selected will not be disclosed. All entrants will be notified of the results via FilmFreeway.

Awards

Awards

All awards are chosen from among the Final Selection films screened on the big screen of Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru on May 4, 2027. The question is not how new the technology is, but how far the story moves the human heart. ＜Selection method under coordination＞

Grand Prix

Grand Prix

Best Film

The summit of all entries. For the one film that, through the power of its story, moved people most deeply on this day. A trophy is awarded.

Jury Prize

Jury Prize

One film chosen by each jury member, in their own name. Not a ranking, but a prize for a personal conviction: "I could not look away from this one."

Sponsor Prize

Sponsor Prize

Awards named by each sponsoring company (up to three) and presented at the awards ceremony. A prize that marks the first encounter between a creator and a company.

SAPPORO Prize

SAPPORO Prize

For a film by a creator with ties to Sapporo, or a film about Sapporo. A prize honoring a story that sets out from this city for the world.

Excellence Award

Excellence Award

Awarded to the theater-screening finalists who travel to the venue in Sapporo on the festival day. A prize for carrying your story all the way to this city.

Award-winning films are released online after the festival through the official channel and elsewhere. After a one-week early screening for crowdfunding supporters (starting the day after the festival), they are released to the public.

Finalists & On-site Award

Finalists (Final Selection films) are chosen on the basis of the work alone, regardless of whether the creator plans to attend. Finalist films are screened on the big screen of Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru on the day.

  • The Excellence Award is given to finalists who attend in person on the festival day.
  • The Grand Prix, Jury Prize, Sponsor Prize, and SAPPORO Prize are judged and awarded regardless of whether the creator attends in person.
  • Final Selection films are credited in the closing credits following the screening.
Call for Films

Eligible Works & Format

There is a single category. Narrative, art, music videos, animation, documentary, genres that don't have a name yet. The only question is whether your story moves the human heart.

The following is an outline as of today. Eligible works, format, and schedule are subject to change. Detailed entry requirements and procedures will be published on this page once finalized.

Main Selection

Main Selection

Across all genres, we select new stories born from the creativity of AI and humans together.

Runtime3–15 minutes / Aspect ratio16:9 (1080p or higher recommended)

Narrative, art, music video, animation, documentary, experimental… any genre is welcome (content must be suitable for all ages). Any film created primarily through AI generation is eligible. Combining it with some live-action footage is fine. Any language, including invented ones (films not in Japanese, or with unnatural Japanese, must include Japanese subtitles).

Eligibility
Open to all nationalities, ages, professionals and amateurs alike. Individuals and teams welcome. Minors must obtain the consent of a parent or guardian.
Eligible works
Films made with generative AI as the primary production method. Partial live-action or a mix with live-action is acceptable. Prior screenings at other festivals or public release do not affect eligibility.
Entry fee
$10
Paid on FilmFreeway (in US dollars).
File format
MP4 (H.264), 1920×1080 or higher recommended, 16:9, stereo audio
Selected films may be asked to submit a screening master.
Subtitles
Films not in Japanese, or with unnatural Japanese, must include Japanese subtitles. English subtitles alongside are recommended for international jury members and attendees.
Number of entries
One film per person (or team). Up to two films may be submitted.
