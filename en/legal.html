2Disclosure under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions

No sales or payments take place on this website. Ticket presales are processed as pledges through crowdfunding (CAMPFIRE); general ticket sales and Online Archive (streaming) sales are processed on teket; and film entry fees are processed on FilmFreeway. The disclosures and terms of each service apply. The following is the disclosure made by the organizer.

Payment method and timing
As stipulated by each external service (credit card, etc.; payment at the time of purchase, pledge, or entry)
Delivery
Presale (CAMPFIRE): after the project closes, a teket redemption code is sent in December 2026 (choose your seat and issue your ticket on teket). Other rewards are delivered at the times stated on the project page. Ember Numbers are posted on the Supporters page once the pledge is confirmed.
General sale (teket): an electronic ticket (reserved seat) is issued on teket upon completion of payment.
Online Archive (digital product): streamed via teket after the festival. Streaming period approx. one month.
Entry fee (FilmFreeway): the entry is accepted upon completion of payment.
Cancellation and refunds
Presale (CAMPFIRE): pledges are non-refundable. Handling is subject to the CAMPFIRE terms of service.
General sale (teket): cancellations and refunds after purchase are not accepted. If the organizer cancels the festival, ticket prices are refunded in accordance with teket's procedures.
Online Archive (streaming): due to the nature of digital products, cancellations and refunds after purchase are not accepted.
Film entries (FilmFreeway): entry fees are not refunded for cancellation after submission.
