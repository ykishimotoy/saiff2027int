Philosophy

A compass, and a festival flame, for an age in which the power to create is open to everyone.

Concept

The Sapporo International AI Film Festival is a flame.

A festival flame for experiencing and honoring the stories born from people who have taken up new ways to create,
and for kindling, from those stories, the next creators and the next stories to come.

Since the dawn of time, humans have carried stories in their heads.

A landscape never seen.
A person who does not yet exist.
A memory that won't fade.
A feeling that words can't hold.
A world you want to show someone.

Imagining has always been something anyone could do. Giving it form was another matter. Cameras, actors, crews, music, editing, CG, time, money. However vivid the world in your head, only a few could finish it as a film and deliver it to someone else.

Now, AI has broken that wall down. One person creates images from their own imagination. Creates sound. Creates voices. Brings characters to life. And raises a single story into being.

An age is beginning in which more people than ever can give their stories form.

What excites us is not AI itself. It's that people who always wanted to create but never had the means have become people who can. A student. An office worker. A musician, a painter, an engineer. Someone who has never made a film in their life can now turn the story inside them into moving images.

Not because AI creates in place of humans. Because the range over which the creativity sleeping inside a person can be carried out into the world has widened like never before.

The more widely the power to create is opened, the more one question matters.

What do you want to create?

You can make beautiful images. You can paint a vast world. You can finish a film alone. That is a real and enormous change. But that alone doesn't move people. Why tell this story? What feeling do you want to leave with the viewer? The more technology we can use, the more human the question that remains at the end.

So what we are truly looking for is not the category of "AI film" itself. It might be a film. It might be animation. It might be music, a game, an interactive experience. The form of expression can change. The technology can change.

At the center of it all stands a human being trying to tell someone something.

Film is not something you merely "watch." In the dark, a world rises on the big screen. Sound resonates through your body. Someone beside you gasps, laughs, cries. Same time, same place, one story shared with a crowd. There are moments, possible only in a cinema, when you experience a story with your whole body.

Precisely because streaming lets us reach films anytime, anywhere, a festival still has meaning. When a film arrives at that moment, the technology recedes into the background. What remains is the work, the story, and whatever is born between the person who made it and the person who received it.

What we want to see is not a race of technical specs.

What will people create, now that they hold a new power?
And how far can those stories move the human heart?

What will you create?
Show us. We'll watch every single one.

Unleash your story. Shake the soul.
The ember is already in your hands.The Sapporo International AI Film Festival opens.

Structure

Structure of the Festival

Screenings, talks, and the reception are designed not as independent events but as a single flow.

I — Screening & Awards

Bathe in Stories

Film Festival (Screenings & Awards)

The Final Selection films are screened on the big screen of the hitaru theater and are eligible for the awards. The Second Selection films are screened (free of charge) at SCARTS. Award-winning films are released online after the festival. We experience films gathered from all over the world together with the audience, taking in each work whole, with the laughter, the silence, the surprise, and the tension of the room. Not simply watching, but bathing in the story.

In the judging, what matters is not merely which AI or technology was used, but what the work depicts, what kind of experience it creates, and how far it moves the audience.

II — Talks

Know Your Creativity

Creator Talks

Film directors, animators, video artists, AI creators: people who actually use AI in film production share how they think, what they struggle with, and what they have tried.

This is not a lecture where you listen to a famous name and go home. By encountering the creativity of others, you come to know your own. Our aim is that between arriving and leaving, your list of "things I might be able to make, too" has grown a little.

III — Meetup

Ignite the Future

Networking Reception

Creators meet creators, of course, but also film meets music, creators meet companies, students meet professionals, Japan meets the world. People who usually work in separate places come together.

Not a mere social gathering, but the first step toward igniting the future. Film and robotics, video and tourism: from encounters that make you think "I never knew those could connect," things that did not yet exist that day begin to move.

& Incubation

A film festival(cinema),
and an incubator(incubation).

The "& Incubation" in our name is not decoration. At this festival, we use "Incubation" to mean four things together: the talks (including live demonstrations), the Networking Reception, the online release of the award-winning films, and the cycle that leads to the next work.

Someone who came as an audience member is one day making films of their own. Two people who met at the reception are watching the film they made together on the screen. We hope to set that cycle in motion. Not just a place that gathers finished films and screens them, but a place where you experience stories, come to know your own creativity, and the next act of creation begins to move.

  1. Talks — Begin creating

    Encounter the ideas and the trial and error of creators, with live demonstrations. For first-timers, a moment of "maybe I can do this too" and a nudge toward that first film.

  2. Networking Reception — Meet, team up

    Creators, companies, students, and international guests meet, and the next film, team, job, or project begins.

  3. Online Release of Award-Winning Films — Deliver

    After the festival, the award-winning films are released online through the official channel and elsewhere. The stories reach even those who couldn't be there, and open the creator's next opportunity.

  4. The Cycle to the Next Work — Return to the screen

    From encounters here, the next film is born and appears on another screen somewhere. We aim for a cycle in which audiences become creators.

Organizer

Organizers & Executive Committee

Sapporo International AI Film Festival Executive Committee

A volunteer-run non-profit executive committee that plans and operates the Sapporo International AI Film Festival. It brings together people on fire to make the future of film that AI has opened up a reality here in Sapporo.

Sapporo Sugoi AI Kai

An AI community based in Sapporo. Under the concept "Experience and share how amazing AI is," it holds this festival and events such as the Sapporo Sugoi AI Matsuri in the city.
