Tickets & Prices

Admission to the theater screening and awards ceremony is ¥1,000, reserved seating (two levels). The Selection Screening at SCARTS (Court and Studio A / B) is free and requires no ticket. The presale runs through crowdfunding (CAMPFIRE, late October to late November); general sale is via teket (listing opens in December, full sale from April 15, 2027). There are no day passes or student tickets.

  • The Networking Reception is open only to those who apply through the presale (crowdfunding, 50 seats) and invited guests. It is not available in general sale.
  • The Selection Screening is a free screening of the Second Selection films at SCARTS Court and Studio A / B (from 10:00; Court until 13:00, Studio A & B until 21:00). No ticket needed.
  • Seats are reserved. In general sale (teket), you choose your seat at purchase. Crowdfunding supporters will receive a teket redemption code in December. Choose your seat on teket, enter the code, and issue your ticket (no additional charge).
  • There are no day passes or student tickets.
  • Award-winning films are released online after the festival through the official channel and elsewhere. Crowdfunding supporters get a one-week early screening starting the day after the festival.
  • Crowdfunding tiers (¥10,000, ¥50,000, etc.) are "support plus rewards," not admission fees. Any tickets included as rewards are described with their face value stated.

Before you come

Before You Come

Subtitles
All films are screened with Japanese and English subtitles.
Interpretation
No interpreters are provided at the talks or the Networking Reception. For conversation, feel free to use AI translation such as ChatGPT on your smartphone.
Online Archive
The talks are recorded and streamed online via teket after the festival (¥1,000, about one month). The Sponsor Session is not included in the archive.
Release of award-winning films
Award-winning films are released online after the festival through the official channel and elsewhere. Crowdfunding supporters get a one-week early screening starting the day after the festival.
Seating
Reserved seating (two levels). Choose your seat on teket. Crowdfunding supporters receive a redemption code in December. If you require wheelchair seating, please contact us in advance.
Age restrictions
There are no age restrictions. Dedicated seating is provided so that preschool children may also attend. Only films suitable for all ages are screened.
Cancellation
Presale (CAMPFIRE): support payments are non-refundable. General sale (teket): cancellations and refunds after purchase are not accepted, except where the organizer cancels the event. Online Archive (streaming): as a digital product, it cannot be cancelled or refunded after purchase. See the Disclosure under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions for details.
Photography
Photography and recording during screenings are prohibited. Photography for documentation and publicity will take place in the venue.
Sponsor presence
No commercials or corporate presentations are shown in the hitaru auditorium or on its screen. Sponsor presence in the theater takes three forms only: the presentation of the Sponsor Prizes at the awards ceremony, the closing credits after the screening (names of sponsors and supporters), and the sponsor booths in the foyer. Sponsor commercials are shown within the Selection Screening loop at SCARTS.
Venue & Access

Venue & access

Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru /
Sapporo Cultural Arts Community Center SCARTS

Sapporo Community Plaza, Kita 1-jo Nishi 1-chome, Chuo-ku, Sapporo 060-0001, Japan
The theater and SCARTS are in the same building, the Sapporo Community Plaza. The hitaru theater is on 4F (1,686 seats on two levels); SCARTS Court is on 1F.

Floors
hitaru theater: entrance on 4F (theater screening & awards ceremony / two levels, reserved seating)
SCARTS Court: 1F (Selection Screening, Sponsor Session, talks, Networking Reception)
SCARTS Studio A & B: 2F (Selection Screening 10:00–21:00)
Theater foyer: 4F (sponsor booths)
SCARTS Mall A & B: 1F (sponsor booths & signage)
Subway
Direct underground walkway from Odori Station (Tozai, Namboku, and Toho lines)
JR
About 10 minutes on foot from the South Exit of Sapporo Station
From the airport
New Chitose Airport → JR Rapid Airport train to Sapporo Station, about 40 minutes
Parking
There is no dedicated parking. Please use public transportation.
