Concept

The Sapporo International AI Film Festival is a flame. A festival flame for experiencing and honoring the stories born from people who have taken up new ways to create, and for kindling, from those stories, the next creators and the next stories to come.

One day, one flow

Bathe in Stories. Know Your Creativity. Ignite the Future.

Screenings, talks, and the reception are designed not as separate events but as a single flow. A day that doesn't end with watching, but leads to the next act of creation.

  1. I

    12:00 – 15:00

    Bathe in Stories

    Theater Screening & Awards Ceremony (hitaru)

    The Final Selection films, on the theater's big screen. Take in each film whole, with the laughter, the silence, and the gasps of the audience around you. Followed by an awards ceremony honoring outstanding films and their creators. Second Selection films screen all day at SCARTS, free of charge.

  2. II

    16:30 – 17:45

    Know Your Creativity

    Creator Talks (SCARTS Court)

    Creators who use AI in real film production share their ideas and their trial and error. Encounter the creativity of others, and go home with a slightly longer list of "things I might be able to make, too."

  3. III

    17:45 – 19:00

    Ignite the Future

    Networking Reception (Incubation)

    Creators and companies, students and professionals, Japan and the world. From encounters that make you think "I never knew those could connect," the next film, team, or job begins to move.

After the festival, the award-winning films will be released online through the official channel and elsewhere. The stories reach even those who couldn't be in the room.
