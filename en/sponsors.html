Why sponsor

Why Sponsor

This is not sponsorship where logos simply line up. The festival is designed as a place where companies meet creators, and where the next job and the next culture begin.

01

Meet the people who have "finished a film with AI," all in one day

From entries submitted around the world, we invite the Final Selection creators to Sapporo. The people gathered here are those who have taken a film all the way to completion using AI. The Networking Reception, the booths in the theater foyer, the Sponsor Session. Whether you're hiring, commissioning, or co-creating, you'll meet people who speak your language, all on the same day.

02

Plant the "first flag" of AI film culture in Sapporo, with us

Filmmaking with generative AI is at the very moment of emerging as a culture. The Sapporo International AI Film Festival is its first edition. Only in the first few editions can you be on the side that, ten years from now, is said to be where Japan's AI film scene spread from. This is not an ad buy. It is an investment remembered as a founding act of culture. Your company's name remains in the closing credits after the screening.

03

Everyone you reach is a stakeholder

Some 1,000 people looking up at the big screen of Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru. People who watch the talk archive to the end. International entrants visiting our multilingual website. You reach only those who are making something with AI, want to, or want others to. This is reach in deep context, a different quality from the "caught my eye somehow" exposure of mass advertising.

Who comes to the venue

AI creators; film, music, and game creators; students; engineers; corporate staff in new business, creative, and recruiting roles; film fans. The hitaru theater (1,686 seats on two levels) operates with reserved seating at ¥1,000 admission. The Selection Screening at SCARTS is open free of charge, so the films and your commercial reach even those without a ticket.

Who we reach beyond the venue

The talks are recorded and streamed as an Online Archive after the festival. Award-winning films are released online on the official channel. The official website is multilingual and reaches international entrants and the global AI film community.

Notes

General Terms

On sponsor presence in the theater — No advertising, commercials, or corporate presentations are shown in the hitaru auditorium or on its screen. We keep advertising out of the room people pay to sit in. Sponsor presence in the theater takes three forms only: the presentation of the Sponsor Prizes, the closing credits after the screening, and the booths in the foyer, provided according to each plan. Commercials and presentations take place at SCARTS.

On commercial videos — Commercials are shown within the loop of the Selection Screening running all day at SCARTS Court and the Studios. They are not shown in the hitaru auditorium or on its screen.
