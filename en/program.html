I — Screening & Awards

Bathe in Stories | Theater Screening & Awards Ceremony

The Final Selection films (finalists), on the big screen of Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru. From 12:00 to 15:00, from the first screening to the awards ceremony. Take in the laughter, the silence, and the gasps of the room, and bathe in the stories.

The Final Selection films (finalists) will be announced on April 15, 2027.

Date & time
May 4, 2027 (Tue, national holiday), 12:00–15:00 (doors 11:30)
Venue
Sapporo Cultural Arts Theater hitaru (two levels, reserved seating)
Flow
Screening of the finalist films → closing credits (names of sponsors and supporters) → awards ceremony
Awards
Grand Prix / Jury Prize / Sponsor Prize / SAPPORO Prize / Excellence Award
Subtitles
Japanese and English subtitles
Admission
¥1,000, reserved seating

Award-winning films are released online after the festival (official YouTube channel and elsewhere). Crowdfunding supporters get a one-week early screening starting the day after the festival.

II — Talks

Know Your Creativity | Creator Talks

This is not a lecture where you listen to a famous name and go home. It is time to come to know your own creativity by encountering the creativity of others.

Creators who use AI in real production share their process from idea to finished work, along with what they struggled with and tried along the way. Some talks may include live demonstrations.

May 4, 2027 (Tue, national holiday), 16:30–17:45

III — Meetup / Incubation

Ignite the Future | Networking Reception

The Networking Reception is the core of the "& Incubation" in this festival's name. Not a mere social gathering, but the first step toward igniting the future.

Telling someone, "I loved your film." Asking, "Could we make something together?" A company exploring new work or support for a creator. From small conversations like these, the next film, team, job, or project begins.

A work born there one day returns to the screen. This is where that cycle begins.

Encounters that make you think, "I never knew those could connect."

Creator × Creator

Film and music, animation and robotics.

Creator × Company

New work, co-creation, and support.

Student × Professional

The push toward a first step.

Japan × World

Film and tourism, from Sapporo to the world.

Date & time
May 4, 2027 (Tue, national holiday), 17:45–19:00
Venue
SCARTS Court (1F)
Attendance
Early supporters (CAMPFIRE Full Access, 50 seats) and invited guests only. Not available in general sale. Finalist creators, jury members, speakers, and sponsoring companies attend.
Program
Finalist creators, jury members, speakers, and sponsoring companies mingle in the same venue. The Sponsor Prizes are presented at the awards ceremony in the theater.
Language
Japanese and English. No interpreters are provided. For conversation, feel free to use AI translation such as ChatGPT on your smartphone.
